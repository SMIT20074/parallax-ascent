01 · Valley

Ascent

It starts at sunrise, at the bottom of a quiet valley. Scroll, and everything around you begins to move at a different speed.

Altitude: 0 m

02 · Peaks

Above the treeline

The far ridges barely drift while the near ones race past. That difference is what your eyes read as depth.

Mount Everest: about 8,850 m

03 · Clouds

Through the cloud deck

Three layers of cloud pass at three speeds, and the wisps closest to you rush by the fastest.

Most clouds sit below 6 km

04 · Stratosphere

The air gets thin

The sky darkens, the first stars appear, and a lone weather balloon floats up beside you.

Jets cruise at about 11 km

05 · Orbit

You made it.

Stars, a moon, a satellite and the curve of the Earth, all in layers that move apart as you scroll.

The ISS orbits at about 400 km
